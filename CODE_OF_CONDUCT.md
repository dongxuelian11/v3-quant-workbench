# Code of conduct

V3 contributors and participants are expected to keep project spaces respectful, technically focused, and safe.

- Treat people with patience and professional respect regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.
- Critique ideas and code with concrete evidence; do not harass, threaten, demean, or disclose private information.
- Do not post credentials, personal data, private market data, proprietary strategies, or security-sensitive reproduction details in public channels.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about scope, especially the boundary between Demo behavior and formal financial capability.
- Report unacceptable behavior or sensitive incidents through the private project channel described in `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior creates safety, privacy, legal, or collaboration risk. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and avoid exposing reporters or affected people.
